Helpful Tips

Can't log in?

Check that your username and password are entered correctly. Passwords are case-sensitive. If you're still unable to sign in, use the password reset option.

Trade order not showing?

Check your order history and verify the order status. Some orders may remain pending until the market is open or the requested price is reached.

Stock price looks different?

Market prices can change quickly. Check the timestamp associated with a quote to determine when the displayed price was last updated.

Investment balance changed?

Investment values fluctuate as market prices change. Review your holdings and recent transactions for additional details.

Protect your account

Never share your password or verification codes. Hello World Financial will never call and ask you to provide your password.

See something suspicious?

If you notice a transaction or trade you don't recognize, contact our Fraud & Security team immediately.

!

Security Reminder

Never provide your password, authentication code, or other sensitive account information to someone who contacts you unexpectedly. If you're unsure whether a communication is legitimate, contact Hello World Financial directly.
